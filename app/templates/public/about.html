🌊 About NHPC Hydro Power & NDMA GeoAlert System

This enterprise platform unifies high-resolution **IMD Mausamgram 0.125° Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP)** forecast analysis with **NDMA Sachet Common Alerting Protocol (CAP 1.2)** real-time disaster warnings.

Engineered for **NHPC power stations across India**, the system tracks catchment hydro-curves, evaluates extreme precipitation (>100mm/24h) and gale wind events, and computes sub-kilometer spatial geofencing using Shapely & PyProj ellipsoidal math.
